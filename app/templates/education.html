🛡️ Essential Rules for Safe Browsing

1. Read Domains from Right to Left

Attackers place trusted brand names at the start of a link (e.g. paypal.com.verify-user.xyz). Always locate the root domain just before the first single forward slash /.

2. Watch for the '@' Symbol

The @ symbol treats everything to its left as a username. https://google.com@phishing.com takes you straight to phishing.com!

3. Raw IP Addresses are Red Flags

Legitimate commercial websites almost never ask users to sign in on raw numerical addresses like http://192.0.2.1/login.

4. Type URLs Manually or Use Bookmarks

Never click sensitive links received via unexpected emails or SMS text messages. Navigate to banking or portal sites through saved bookmarks.
